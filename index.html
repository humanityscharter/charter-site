§ 1.0 Founder's Proposed Floor

The Foundation of the Charter

The founder puts forward these three commitments as a starting floor for debate.

Principle 01

Liberate Instead of Displace

The gains of automation must reach the people whose labor, knowledge, and lives built them. We reject techno-feudalism where a few monopolize compute while the many are economically discarded.

Proposed Floor Standard
Principle 02

A Generation-Scale Goal

We should work toward abundance within a generation. This proposal sets an ambitious 30-year roadmap with draft targets and milestones across legal, physical, and local/regional domains. Progress depends on evidence, democratic decisions, and local readiness; Year 30 is a planning horizon, not a promised endpoint.

Proposed 30-Year Goal
Principle 03

Universal Global Equity

The transition cannot belong to Silicon Valley or wealthy nation states alone. People across languages, regions, and disciplines should help shape the transition from the start.

Global Access Mandate
§ 2.0 System Architecture

The Eight Systems of Abundance

The Charter treats material scarcity largely as a problem of system design. It proposes eight interdependent systems for shared abundance.

Ecological sustainability is a binding cross-cutting boundary for all eight systems.

§ 5.0 Deliberative Mechanism

Open to everyone, shaped through debate, and built together.

How the Charter changes is open for discussion and development. Discord is the temporary Governance Incubator for Forum discussion and live debate. The permanent governance and voting platform has not been decided, and no binding amendment vote is open. Discord polls and chapter reactions are advisory, not binding votes. GitHub stores Charter documents and their change history only.

1. Proposed Foundation

The Foundation is the founder's proposal, not a ratified or locked core. The community can debate and shape it.

2. Proposal Tier

Ideas across all 26 chapters are open for debate and revision. Anyone may propose an amendment or counter-proposal.

3. Open Questions

Unanswered questions and design gaps. A position can be brought forward for discussion as a proposal.

§ 6.0 Share a Proposal Idea

Suggest a Change

No software engineering skills needed. Submit a serious idea for private human review without creating an account. A reviewer may manually post a redacted proposal to Discord when it is useful for discussion. A submission is not a vote, public post, or approved amendment.

Keep your receipt to check status here. An email address is stored only when you ask for private follow-up.

20–4,000 characters.

Your proposal is stored privately for review. Your name or alias, optional email address, original proposal, rationale, and private reviewer notes are deleted from the active proposal database when a final review decision is recorded.

Unresolved submissions have a seven-day private-data retention limit. Automated cleanup may close review up to an hour early. If a storage failure prevents deletion, private review and new submissions remain unavailable until cleanup is verified.

Deleted information may remain temporarily in restricted system-recovery history before automatically expiring. After a backup restore, original private submissions and private notes from that backup are removed before private review or new submissions resume. Receipt and status information, a redacted public proposal, and a discussion link may remain. For privacy or contact requests, email hello@humanityscharter.org.

Proposal submission and receipt lookup are unavailable until secure setup completes.
