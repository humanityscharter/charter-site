# Contributing to Humanity's Charter

> "Open to everyone, shaped through debate, built together."

The Charter is a starting position. You do not have to agree with it to help shape it. Read it, point out what does not work, bring evidence or lived experience, and offer a better way forward.

## Where discussion happens

- **Discord** is the temporary Governance Incubator for Forum discussion and live debate. Join it at [discord.gg/tBrZFgs23K](https://discord.gg/tBrZFgs23K). The permanent governance and voting platform has not been decided.
- **GitHub** stores Charter documents and their change history only. It is not a place to debate, cast votes, or ratify amendments.
- **Website chapter reactions** show reader engagement. Agree, Disagree, and Propose a Change are not formal amendment ballots.

## What can happen at launch

The public site can launch, people can challenge the Charter, and governance can be worked out in public. No core text or voting rule has been ratified yet. No binding Charter-amendment vote is open. A Discord poll or a chapter reaction can help show opinion, but it is advisory.

## How to contribute now

1. Name the chapter, passage, or governance question you want to discuss.
2. Explain what you think is missing or wrong and why it matters.
3. If you have a replacement idea, put it forward. Say what you are unsure about as well.
4. Take part in the [Discord Governance Incubator](https://discord.gg/tBrZFgs23K). It is a temporary place for Forum discussion and live debate; live attendance is not required.

No one has to be a developer or use GitHub to join the debate. The rules for formal voting, including who can vote, are not being assumed here. They are part of the work ahead. See `GOVERNANCE.md` for the launch rule and `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` for how we treat each other while disagreeing.
