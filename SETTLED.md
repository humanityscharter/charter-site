# Founder's Proposed Principles, Not Yet Settled

> "Open to everyone, shaped through debate, built together."

This file records ideas the founder put forward so there is something real to debate. **No community has ratified a settled core.** Nothing here is locked, and this file does not authorize a vote. The launch rule is in `GOVERNANCE.md`.

## Starting direction

**Liberate, not displace.** The Charter argues that the gains from automation should let people live well rather than push them out of economic life. The 26 chapters offer a direction and a set of proposals, not a finished answer.

## Ideas for how the Charter might be organized

- A place for principles that a future community has actually ratified.
- A place for proposals that people are debating and revising.
- A place for open questions where the Charter does not yet have an answer.

These are proposed categories, not settled tiers or a protected Charter core.

## Equal participation is an aim, not a solved voting system

The founder wants people who shape the Charter to have an equal voice. The community still needs to decide who is eligible for a formal vote and how to prevent one person from casting more than one. An email address, a GitHub account, or a website reaction does not settle that question.

The ideas in this file are starting proposals, not launch rules. People can debate them, change them, or reject them.

The public site may launch and governance may be debated in the temporary [Discord Governance Incubator](https://discord.gg/tBrZFgs23K), for Forum discussion and live debate. **No binding Charter-amendment vote is open.** The permanent governance and voting platform has not been decided. GitHub stores Charter documents and their change history only.
