# Governance at launch

> **Current launch rule:** Discord is the temporary Governance Incubator for Forum discussion and live debate. The permanent governance and voting platform has not been decided. No binding Charter-amendment vote is open. Discord polls and chapter reactions are advisory, not binding votes. GitHub stores Charter documents and their change history only. The website proposal form sends ideas to private human review; a reviewer may manually post a redacted proposal to Discord when it is useful for discussion.

## What applies now

- The Charter and the founder's ideas are starting proposals. No community has ratified a settled core or a voting process.
- The founder's temporary participation rules are in `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`. The founder remains accountable and may appoint moderators with defined responsibilities. These rules cover conduct in project spaces; they do not open a binding Charter-amendment vote.
- Anyone may join the discussion in the [Governance Incubator on Discord](https://discord.gg/tBrZFgs23K). It is a temporary place to work through the questions, not a claim that the permanent platform has been chosen.
- Governance itself is open for discussion. That includes who participates, how a future process treats people fairly, and how a future binding decision could work.
- Reactions on the website and polls in Discord can show what people think. They do not change the Charter or open a binding vote.
- The website form is for sharing an idea without making it public first. A human reviews it privately. If it is selected for public discussion, a reviewer may manually post a redacted version to Discord.
- GitHub keeps the Charter documents and their change history. It is not the place to debate, vote, or ratify an amendment.

This document describes the current launch rules. Governance proposals can be questioned and revised through the temporary Discord Governance Incubator.
