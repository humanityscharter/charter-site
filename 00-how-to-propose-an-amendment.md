# How to propose a change

You found something in the Charter you want to change. That is welcome. The chapters are a starting position, not a settled constitution.

## Bring the idea into the open

For now, bring the idea to the [Discord Governance Incubator](https://discord.gg/tBrZFgs23K), the temporary place for Forum discussion and live debate. Say which chapter and passage you mean, what you would change, and why. You can bring a source, your own experience, or both. A counter-proposal can replace an idea rather than merely adjust its wording. The permanent governance and voting platform has not been decided.

The website's current form sends an idea to private human review. It does not file a public proposal or record a formal vote. A reviewer may manually post a redacted version to Discord when it is useful for discussion. Do not mistake a receipt for an official docket.

## What happens after discussion?

The community still needs to work out the governance process. A Discord poll can help show what people think, but it is advisory. **No binding Charter-amendment vote is open.**

The permanent governance and voting platform has not been decided. GitHub stores Charter documents and their change history only. Website chapter reactions are separate and advisory; they do not count as amendment votes.

See `GOVERNANCE.md` for the launch rule and `CONTRIBUTING.md` for ways to take part.
